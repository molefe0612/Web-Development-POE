About Us

Our History

Founded in 2026, TS-lets Confectionary grew from a passionate home bakery venture into a fully formed local confectionery store. We specialize in bringing sweet visions to life through high-quality premium baking craft.


Mission & Vision Statements

Our Mission: To bake joy into the heart of our community by providing freshly prepared, high quality hand-crafted custom cakes.

Our Vision: The go-to custom bakery and confectionery destination in Mankweng and the greater Polokwane region.
